System Architecture & Algorithmic Formulation

1. Dynamic Green Light Allocation Math

Traditional traffic lights operate on fixed cycles (e.g. 30 seconds per lane), causing severe bottlenecks when one lane is congested and others are empty. AI-STMS continuously computes the weighted Passenger Car Unit (PCU) density across all approaches:

Density_i = (N_cars * 1.0) + (N_bus * 2.5) + (N_truck * 3.0) + (N_bike * 0.5) + (N_emergency * 50.0)
Urgency_i = Density_i + (Max_Wait_Time_i * k_starvation)
T_green = clamp( T_min, (Urgency_i / Sum_Urgency) * T_cycle, T_max )

2. Emergency Vehicle Priority (Green Corridor)

When an emergency vehicle (Ambulance, Fire Engine, Police) is detected via computer vision or emergency transponders, the preemption manager immediately initiates a rapid safe clearance sequence (2-second yellow on current green lane, followed by an immediate override to GREEN on the emergency approach while all conflicting lanes are locked RED).

3. Environmental & Fuel Waste Mitigation

By reducing idling stop-and-go delays by an average of 35-45%, AI-STMS substantially cuts down urban fuel consumption and carbon footprint, supporting sustainable and smart city development.
